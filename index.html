01 / WHY

Hindi man nagkatuluyan,
bakit hindi tayo magtulungan?

“Service isn't really about the position or recognition. It's about being willing to give your time, effort, and heart to others.”

I want to continue serving my fellow youth. Through my experiences in different ministries and youth organizations, I learned that serving is not about the title, but about showing up and doing your part.

If given the opportunity, I want to serve with you—makinig sa inyo, matuto kasama kayo, take responsibility, and help whenever I can.

— Carl
